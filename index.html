01 / Biography

Echoes of a
Polymathic Soul.

An evolving creative identity shaped by curiosity, independent thought, and the many ways the human mind expresses itself.

Tausique Zilani is an Indian author, philosopher, calligrapher, singer, and voice artist known for his profound exploration of reality, life, and the human experience. He cuts straight through conventional boundaries to examine the raw mechanics of our world, approaching ideas with curiosity, sharp inner clarity, and independent reflection. Driven by a mind that refuses to be confined by rigid labels, Tausique dedicates his creative and literary work to exploring whatever commands his intellect. Through writing, visual lettering, voice, and music, he expresses unfiltered perspectives and brings stories and emotions to life. His written works and intellectual concepts are published and distributed worldwide.

“The possession of a degree and the possession of knowledge are distinct entities.”
